# BusinessOS AI User Flow

## First-Time User Journey

1. User Registration
2. Email Verification
3. Business Creation
4. Business Knowledge Hub Setup
5. AI Workspace

---

## Registration

The user creates an account using:

- Full Name
- Email Address
- Password

---

## Business Creation

The user creates the business by entering:

- Business Name
- Industry
- Logo
- Phone Number
- Address
- Working Hours
- Timezone
- Currency

---

## Business Knowledge Hub

The AI guides the business owner through a setup wizard to collect business knowledge.

Knowledge may come from:

- Manual input
- FAQs
- Services
- Products
- Business Rules
- Policies
- PDF
- Word
- Excel
- Images

---

## AI Workspace

After onboarding, the user is redirected to the AI Workspace where BusinessOS AI becomes the primary interface for daily operations.
